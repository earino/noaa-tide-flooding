## The source's terms - public domain, preserved

NOAA CO-OPS data is a **US Government work in the public domain**, verified by reading the CO-OPS
portal's own disclaimers page (`https://tidesandcurrents.noaa.gov/disclaimers.html`, read
2026-09-19): *"The information on government servers are in the public domain, unless specifically
annotated otherwise, and may be used freely by the public."* The same page requests attribution and
carries a constraint we honour in full:

> *"This information shall not be modified in content and then presented as official government
> material."*

Therefore: **this is a derived dataset and is not an official NOAA, NOS or NWS product.** The
derivation is labelled as such in the card, the data dictionary and the manifest. Attribution is
given as requested, not because it is required.

Attribution: NOAA National Ocean Service, Center for Operational Oceanographic Products and Services
(CO-OPS), verified water level observations and flood thresholds, <https://tidesandcurrents.noaa.gov/>,
accessed 2026-09-18.

Suggested citation: NOAA CO-OPS. *Verified daily maximum water level observations (`daily_max_min`)
and minor flood thresholds for 122 NOS stations, 2006-2025.* Retrieved 2026-09-18. Public Domain.
Derived dataset: *NOAA tide flooding prediction dataset, version 2026.09.*

Corroborating evidence for the public-domain status: `nauticalcharts.noaa.gov/data/data-licensing.html`
records NOAA data released under a CC0-1.0 public-domain dedication, and the NCEI ISO metadata for
CO-OPS stations carries the same CC0 waiver.
